  of psychology at the University of Buffalo and explicitly records OSS

  independently affiliates him with the university. The identity is confirmed

  employment, not as his immediate pre-OSS affiliation or last civilian
  employer. Yale doctoral evidence is kept as education, not employment.

  uncommon exact name and major rank, identifies him as British, and records
  his Shetland Base and Danish Section commands at SFHQ. The cited locator was
  visually verified at PDF page 154, printed page 36. This confirms identity
  and Allied wartime assignment only; no pre-service employer is inferred.

  worked on undersea weapons in Naples before OSS removed him from Italy in
  1943. Indexed **Alfred Sciarrotta** is therefore a probable match. The first-
  name difference and lack of a unique identifier remain visible. The result
  is published as a qualified occupation-only finding because the Naples
  employer is unnamed; his later Naval Torpedo Station assignment is not
  recast as a pre-OSS employer.

  A Scott, Edward A Scigliano, Frank Sciortino, John W Scoggin, Patrick E
  Scoleri, Phillip Scinto and Richard G Scoffone. The Philip/Phillip spelling
  variant and Army/index grade differences for Scigliano and Scoggin are

- The remaining incomplete or common-name rows received staged exact-name,
  OSS-context and employment-oriented discovery checks. Plausible modern
  namesakes, genealogy-only pages, people-search pages and unrelated records

- CIA automated discovery remained unavailable under the source's robots
