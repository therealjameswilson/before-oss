## Review method

PDF pages 87 and 88 were rendered at 150 dpi and visually inspected. Every
printed name, initial, suffix, private identifier or blank, box and location in
the ten-row cohort was compared with the immutable database. Charles J Collins
Jr., Gene Collins, Jerry Collins, John H Collins and Joseph G Collins carry
eight-digit identifiers. They remain research-only and are masked publicly.

The complete official Army merged file was restored from NARA, verified at
185,043,578 compressed bytes and SHA-256
`80c46e83937c6483d11def47fbb1629cab0ac135694fdfa09268b66d826f1066`,
and scanned across all 9,200,232 fixed-width records. Each of the five indexed
identifiers selected one row. The matches date Army entry and identify private
entry grade. They confirm enlisted Army identities; they do not establish a
later unit, OSS start date or administrative transfer.

Gene Collins's identifier-matched row carries civilian-occupation value 992 at
Army entry on 17 August 1942. Jerry Collins's row carries 006 on 30 April 1941,
John H Collins's row carries 330 on 27 January 1942, and Joseph G Collins's row
carries 685 on 15 June 1943. The relevant pages of NARA's technical
documentation and code lists were visually and textually checked. The historical
labels are Students; Authors, editors, and reporters; Fruit and vegetable
graders and packers; and Welders and flame cutters. These are medium-confidence,
strongly date-bounded status or occupation observations. They do not name a
school, employer, publication, product, industry, workplace, exact work dates or
precise relationship to OSS service.

Charles J Collins Jr.'s exact match carries occupation value 999. Because the
reviewed documentation does not support a usable substantive occupation from
that value, no occupation claim is published. The index's printed Jr. suffix is
preserved even though the Army name field omits it.

For the identifier-free cases, the Army file has twenty-four exact-name rows for
George E Collins, one for Hugh R Collins, four for Jeremiah J Collins and
fifty-two for John F Collins. It has no exact-name row for Lucille G Collins.
The one Hugh row remains unassigned because a name alone is insufficient. A
1940 Rochester directory entry for a Jeremiah J Collins is likewise not assigned
without a service, residence, birth or OSS bridge. The Army file is not a
women's-service, officer, Navy, Marine Corps or comprehensive OSS roster, so
non-hits are not negative proof.

Every person received recorded NARA and CIA context checks, exact-name OSS and
meaningful variant searches, employment and occupation queries, and applicable
institutional, obituary, directory, newspaper, current Library of Congress and
other archival discovery. Modern architects, people-finder pages, later veterans,
postwar careers and obituary namesakes were rejected. An obituary for a Joseph G
Collins born in 1923 conflicts with the exact identifier-matched Army row's birth-
year field and was not assigned. No authenticated NARA Catalog request was used.
