## John O'Hara Cosgrave II

08 and the civilian category Commercial artists at Army entry on 1 June 1943.

Cosgrave (1908-1968) as a freelance commercial artist and book illustrator. A

The exact private identifier, exact first and last name, middle initial, 1908
birth field, and distinctive occupation support a high-confidence match to
the younger illustrator, John O'Hara Cosgrave II. They also distinguish him
from his older namesake uncle, the newspaper editor John O'Hara Cosgrave
(1866-1947). The accepted claim is documented prewar self-employment as a
freelance commercial artist and book illustrator. It is not marked immediate
pre-OSS or last civilian employment: the accessible sources establish a 1940
credit but do not close the chronology before OSS service.
