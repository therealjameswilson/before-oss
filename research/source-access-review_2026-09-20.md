# CIA Reading Room automated-search access review

Reviewed 2026-09-20 UTC against the [CIA robots.txt](https://www.cia.gov/robots.txt). Its wildcard rule disallows `/readingroom/search/` and specifies a 10-second crawl delay. The pre-existing CIA adapter targeted `/readingroom/search/site/...` with a one-second limiter, so that route is not acceptable for new automated batches under the current policy.

One narrowly scoped page-121 query for Mirko P. Dominis was sent by the legacy adapter before this policy check. It returned HTTP 200 with zero discovery candidates. No response body was preserved. After the policy check, no further CIA search request was sent. The adapter now fetches and parses the host's current robots.txt in memory before a live search, fails closed when the policy is unavailable or disallows the URL, and applies the published crawl delay if a future policy permits the route. Tests use mock robots fixtures and make no live requests. A dry run remains request-free.

Previously recorded CIA request audits remain in the private research database for accountability; their existence does not establish that a person has been fully researched or that a CIA document was reviewed. Future minimum-protocol outcomes must use an accessible equivalent official-source check (for example, an item-specific NARA Catalog or personnel-file source) and separately document any blocked CIA search. Search-result snippets and third-party mirrors remain discovery leads only, not employer evidence. If CIA changes the policy or supplies a sanctioned API, recheck its current terms before re-enabling the route.
