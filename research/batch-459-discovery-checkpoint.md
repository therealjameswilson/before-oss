academic appointment at Washington State College in 1934, his move to a UC

Berkeley in 1945. Barbara Ertter's 2001 *Fremontia* article, drawing on
Constance's oral history, says he was recruited into OSS mere months after his
son's birth in December 1942. The Berkeley oral-history catalog independently
describes Washington State College teaching in 1934-1937 and a UC Berkeley
professorship spanning 1937-1976. UC Berkeley is therefore the best-supported
immediate pre-OSS affiliation and last civilian employer. The exact OSS-entry
date and whether Berkeley formally placed him on leave remain unproved.

Charles Coussoule in Cairo in 1943 and says OSS recruited him for operations in
Greece. The contemporary 18 October 1945 *India-Burma Theater Roundup*
identifies Captain Charles Coussoule as a leader of the American-Kachin Rangers
in an OSS organization. Together with the page 90 Captain row and the separate
page 95 Coussoule row, this supports a high-confidence identity spelling
variant and a possible duplicate group. The sources do not identify his
pre-service employer.
