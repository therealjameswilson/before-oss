## Richard W. Cutler chronology

W. Cutler. An FBI report summarizing Duncan C. Lee's statement says another
young lawyer in Donovan's firm, Richard Cutler, continued as attorney for
Russian War Relief after Lee left New York in June 1942. An independent
profile names the Wall Street firm Donovan, Leisure, Newton and Lombard,
sequences eight months there before entry into the Army Air Force as a second
lieutenant, and then says he transferred to OSS.

- immediate pre-OSS affiliation: United States Army Air Forces, second
  lieutenant, high confidence and explicitly sequential.

The FBI spelling `Lumbard` and magazine spelling `Lombard` are both preserved
in organization metadata. The military assignment is not mislabeled as a
