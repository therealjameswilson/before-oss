## Review method

PDF pages 88 and 89 were rendered at 150 dpi and visually inspected. Every
printed name, initial, rank or blank, private identifier or blank, box and
location in the ten-row cohort was compared with the immutable database. The
index prints `Josephthine M Conde`; that spelling remains recoverable and is
not silently changed to Josephine. Robert S Condie's six-digit number and
Trimble C Condict's seven-digit officer-style number remain literal and
private; neither was padded or substituted with an Army-file value.

The complete official Army merged file was scanned across all 9,200,232
fixed-width records. Its compressed source was previously verified at
185,043,578 bytes with SHA-256
`80c46e83937c6483d11def47fbb1629cab0ac135694fdfa09268b66d826f1066`.
Anthony E Concordia and Vahram H Condayan were the only cohort members with
eligible eight-digit index identifiers. Anthony's identifier produced no hit.
Vahram's identifier and exact name produced one matching row dated 25 October
1944 with grade PVT. Every exact full-name row was also reviewed. George P
Condakes has a single row dated 1 December 1942 with 1922 birth-year and
Massachusetts-residence values. Trimble C Condict has a single row dated 16
July 1941 with 1917 birth-year and Vermont-residence values, but its enlisted
identifier differs from the officer-style number printed in the OSS index and
is not silently substituted. Robert V and Robert E Condie are initial
mismatches for Robert S. Non-hits are not negative proof because the Army file
is incomplete and is not a comprehensive officer, Navy, Marine Corps,
women's-service, foreign-personnel or OSS roster.

The current project CIA adapter ran one bounded official OSS Collection search
for each person and returned no candidates. The current Library of Congress
adapter ran bounded exact-name employment searches. Twelve newspaper-page
candidates were checked at OCR-context level and rejected through the
idempotent review-decision workflow. Two Anthony Concordia candidates combine
`Anthony` and `Concordia` as separate Kansas weather-station place names. Ten E
C Condit candidates name Donald Condit or Mary Condit-Smith, contain broken OCR
for condition, conditions or air-conditioned, or otherwise lack the indexed
name and an OSS bridge. None is published as evidence.

George P Condakes produced one exact-name Army row. Its 1922 birth-year and
Massachusetts-residence values align with a detailed obituary for George Peter
Condakes, born in 1922 and associated with the Boston area. The obituary says
he attended Northeastern University before Army draft and started in the 100th
Infantry Division before joining OSS. This explicit pathway and the unusual
name support high-confidence identity. The 100th Infantry Division is
published as a medium-confidence immediate military affiliation because the
sequence currently rests on one obituary; the Army record corroborates
service but not unit. Northeastern is published separately as student status,
not as an employer. The family business mentioned after the war is excluded
from the pre-OSS model.

Vahram H Condayan's exact private identifier confirms the index-to-Army match.
NARA's direct Entry 219 description independently identifies approximately
100 pages concerning Vahram H. Condayan's intelligence reporting from the
French Somali Coast, circa July 1944-October 1946, at Box 3, WN#27076. This
confirms identity and provides a second archival review target. It does not
name a pre-OSS employer. Because the Army row is dated 25 October 1944, after
the approximate start of NARA's reporting period, its civilian-occupation code
is not represented as pre-OSS evidence. Secondary consular and naturalization
leads remain unpublished pending direct, chronologically relevant sources.

The 1941-1942 University of Vermont catalogue was downloaded from the
institution, and its title page and physical page 293 (printed page 292) were
rendered and visually inspected. The graduate-student register lists Trimble
Chubb Condict, B.S. from Bucknell, in education, with Richmond, Vermont. A
single exact-name Army row carries Vermont residence; the official state-code
page was also rendered and checked. The distinctive full name, state, middle
initial and index rank support high-confidence identity. University of Vermont
is published as documented prewar student status, never employment. The July
1941 Army entry overlaps the catalogue period, so the student affiliation is
not labeled immediate. A cached Detachment 101 promotion transcription aligns
name and first-lieutenant rank, but its current public URL redirects elsewhere;
it is excluded from public evidence rather than cited as stable support.

Charles L Conaughty, Anthony E Concordia, Marie L Concordia, Josephthine M

Every person received recorded NARA index context, official CIA checks,
