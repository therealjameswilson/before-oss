## Research outcomes

- An official OSS personnel bulletin identifies **John R. Schoemer Jr.** as
  assistant general counsel effective September 8, 1944. A Hamilton College
  institutional history dates his association with **Townley & Updike** from
  1939 for more than thirty years. The firm is therefore published with high
  confidence as both his strongly date-bounded immediate affiliation and last
  civilian employer. The evidence does not say he was recruited from the firm
  or establish formal leave terms.
- Two official OSS documents identify **Thomas D. Schocken** as a U.S. Naval
  Reserve lieutenant junior grade in Strategic Intelligence. A Bilbao
  municipal history and a scholarly planning history separately document his
  1933-1936 collaboration in Estanislao Seguróla's studio, earlier work with
  Ernst May's Soviet planning group, and later membership in the British Town
  and Country Planning Association. These are published as prewar
  professional affiliations, not as a claimed immediate employer.
- A University of Colorado institutional profile, corroborated by a reputable
  obituary, identifies **Eddie William Schodt** and explicitly documents brief
  1942 OSS work. His 1938 Valley City bachelor's degree and 1940 University of
  Colorado master's degree are kept as student affiliations rather than
  employers; the 1940-1942 employment gap remains open.
- Official Army data confirms **Solomon Schneyer** at high confidence. An
  image-backed 1941 Temple University School of Chiropody yearbook supports a
  visibly qualified, medium-confidence student affiliation. The yearbook lacks
  a protected identifier or OSS link, so Box 686 review remains necessary.
- Official Army data and a Morning Call obituary identify **Richard T.
  Schoenberger** at high confidence. His Quartermaster Corps service and OSS
  special duty in California support a qualified probable-immediate military
  pathway, not a civilian employer; the transfer date is not stated.
- Eight additional official Army crosswalks support high-confidence identity
  matches for Horace J. Schnelzer, Walter O. Schnyder, Robert J. Schoen, Jerry
  Schoenbaum, Milton C. Schoenheim, William A. Schoepper, Donald R. Schofield
  and George Schofield. Full identifiers, entry-grade fields, occupation codes

- A Navy casualty namesake for Donald Schofield was rejected because branch

  Schnetzky and a Coast Artillery transcription for John F. Schoenack remain
  insufficient identity evidence. Postwar music-industry references for Jerry
