## Source-specific cautions

- The Army merged file is incomplete, especially for officers. A missing row is not negative proof.
- Army occupation codes 684, 992, and 227 identify broad categories, not employers or exact jobs. Civitella's residual metal-production category is not silently equated with his separately documented Ford employment.
- USSOCOM explicitly places Ford work before Civitella's February 1943 Army entry and Army duty at Camp Mackall one week before OSS screening. Ford is therefore the last civilian employer, while the Army is the immediate affiliation.
- Girard College and Pennsylvania Maritime Academy are student affiliations, never employer claims.
- The printed `Iganazio` and `Dutch Ar` forms remain unchanged. Plausible corrections or expansions are research leads only.
- George V Clainos's 86th Infantry roster is not dated relative to OSS and is not promoted to an immediate predecessor assignment.
- Nicholas D Clainos's exact-name candidate remains a separate person because the officer identifier conflicts with the index.
- The Library of Congress title uses `128 Infantry Greek battalion`. That wording is preserved; the project does not silently harmonize it with later `122nd` accounts.
- An OSS personnel-file index row establishes that a file exists, not by itself that every listed person served in OSS. This is especially important for Peter D Clainos.
