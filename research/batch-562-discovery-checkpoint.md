## Accepted outcomes

### Eugene W. DeMoore

A direct May 1944 OSS Board of Officers interview gives Eugene W. DeMoore's
exact name and officer number. The number matches the indexed six-digit value
only after restoring the leading zero printed in the interview; the immutable
index value itself remains unchanged. A May 1943 *Field Artillery Journal*
article independently corroborates his name, rank and branch.

The interview explicitly sequences active Army service from 8 October 1940,
Field Artillery duty, promotion to captain on 1 February 1942 and OSS entry in
October 1943. United States Army Field Artillery is therefore published as his
immediate pre-OSS military assignment, not as a civilian employer. The same
interview states that his civilian occupation was civil engineer in New York
City. Because it names no firm, agency or practice arrangement, the project
publishes the occupation but does not manufacture a last civilian employer.

University dissertation independently identify John De Montis among the

supports a high-confidence wartime identity. Neither source establishes a
pre-OSS employer. A later undated drugstore-proprietor lead remains rejected
pending a contemporary Detroit directory and file comparison.
