## Costa Couvaras pathway

The exact-identifier Army record and an archive-based Ritchie History Museum
account establish a coherent pathway. Couvaras worked in public relations for
the Greek Pavilion at the 1939 New York World's Fair, served as an Athens
correspondent for Assyrmatos in 1939-1940, and became assistant to the publisher
of New York's Greek-language National Herald after returning to the United
States in 1940. He entered the Army on 13 August 1942, trained in military
intelligence at Camp Ritchie, and transferred to OSS headquarters in April
1943.

The database therefore models the United States Army as the immediate pre-OSS
military affiliation and the National Herald as the best-supported last
civilian employer before service. Assyrmatos and the Greek Pavilion remain
earlier documented employment. The damaged Army grade field is not used to
contradict the institutional history's later identification of Couvaras as a
second lieutenant.
