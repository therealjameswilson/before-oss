## Thomas L Curtis

The 1989 U.S. Marine Corps History and Museums Division study *Herringbone
Cloak - GI Dagger: Marines of the OSS* names Gunnery Sergeant, later Warrant
Officer, Thomas L. Curtis. It describes a career Marine who worked in the
Reconnaissance Section of Amphibious Corps, Atlantic at Quantico, instructed
the future 1st Raider Battalion in rubber-boat handling, and was among the
first Marines transferred into OSS paramilitary training. The roster also
lists Thomas L. Curtis among OSS warrant officers.

but the rankless index row supplies no branch or private identifier. The
profile therefore publishes the reconnaissance-section role only as a medium-

pending Box 161 review. Three unrelated exact-name Army rows are not selected.
