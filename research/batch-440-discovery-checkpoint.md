directory, genealogy and similarly named results for the cohort were rejected
when they lacked the printed middle initial, private identifier or direct OSS
connection. An enthusiast-maintained Operational Group roster naming Hugo was
not used as final evidence.

Research for every person covered official NARA and CIA context, exact-name

institutional archives, obituaries, directories, newspapers, current Library
of Congress discovery and other archival searches. Search snippets, unsourced
genealogy, modern people finders, relative-centered material and name-only
biographies were rejected as final evidence. Sensitive modern details were not
retained.

The user-supplied NARA Catalog API credential was not used. No raw Army row,
full private identifier, live API response, home address, phone number,
living-relative detail or unrelated sensitive data is retained.
