## Review method

PDF page 84 was rendered at 150 dpi and inspected before research. Every
printed name, initial, blank or stated rank, private identifier or blank, box,
note and location in the ten-row cohort was compared with the immutable
database. The page visibly prints `Sp 2/C` and `Navy` for Dean A Cline. It
prints no rank for the other nine people. Three eligible private identifiers
were used for direct comparison to the official Army file without retaining or
publishing their values.

The complete Army merged file was scanned across 9,200,232 fixed-width
records. It contained three exact-name Howard J Clifton rows, one William R
Clifton row and one Sheldon R Cline row. The private identifiers selected one
row for each of those people. It contained no exact-name row for Mary F
Clifton, A C Cline, Dean A Cline, Jeraldine E Cline, Ray S Cline, Walter B
Cline or DeWitt Clinton. The latter non-hits are not negative proof: the file
is incomplete and is neither a Navy register nor a comprehensive OSS roster.
The official layout documentation and the two rendered occupation-code pages
were inspected before any selected field was interpreted.

Research then proceeded through NARA index and Director's Office context, CIA
Reading Room and official-document discovery, exact-name and meaningful
variant OSS searches, employment and occupation searches, institutional
archives, obituaries, directories, newspapers and the current Library of
Congress search API. Library of Congress results were broad discovery counts
rather than identity proof: 243 for Howard J Clifton, 516 for Mary F Clifton,
146 for William R Clifton, 679 for A C Cline, 12 for Dean A Cline, one for
Jeraldine E Cline, 284 for Ray S Cline, 42 for Sheldon R Cline, 192 for Walter
B Cline and 2,271 for DeWitt Clinton. No newly reviewed Library of Congress
item established an employer.

The existing Ray S. Cline Papers chronology was re-audited against the current
finding-aid PDF. It continues to support a Harvard junior fellowship in
1941-1942, Department of the Navy cryptanalysis in 1942-1943 and OSS service
beginning in 1943. Harvard remains a professional affiliation, not an employer,
and the Navy role remains a government assignment. Ray's earlier
`verified_employer_found` status was therefore corrected to
`requires_archival_review` without changing the valid affiliation claims.

NARA's Director's Office microfilm roll list was downloaded from the official
site. PDF physical page 137, internal page 134, was rendered at 150 dpi and
visually inspected. Roll 87, frame 3, names `DeWitt Clinton` among people
mentioned for photographs dated 22 August 1944 near Besancon, France. The
exact distinctive name and explicit OSS archival context support a probable
identity lead, but the page supplies no identifier, middle name, occupation,
role, unit or pre-OSS chronology. DeWitt Clinton Poole and other search
collisions were rejected rather than merged.

The user-supplied NARA Catalog API credential was not used. No raw Army row,
full private identifier, live API response, home address, phone number,
relative detail or family-sensitive data is retained. Search snippets,
commercial people finders, unsourced genealogy and name-only biographies were
rejected as final evidence. No access restriction or CAPTCHA was bypassed.

## Source-specific cautions

- The Army merged file is incomplete and is neither an officer register nor a
  Navy file. A non-hit is not negative proof.
- Howard J Clifton's identifier selects one of three exact-name Army rows. The
  selected record dates entry to 30 December 1942 as a private and carries
  occupation value 170. The rendered code page labels 1-70 `Sales clerks`.
  The two identifier-mismatched namesakes are rejected.
- William R Clifton's identifier selects the sole exact-name Army row, dated
  14 March 1942 as a private with occupation value 240. The rendered code page
  labels 2-40 `Attendants, recreation and amusement, n.e.c.` The catch-all
  historical category names no venue, employer or exact job.
- Sheldon R Cline's identifier selects the sole exact-name Army row, dated 8
  March 1944 as a private with occupation value 180. The rendered code page
  labels 1-80 `Salesmen, to consumers`. It names no product, company or
  workplace.
- Dean A Cline's enlisted naval classification comes only from the visibly
  printed `Sp 2/C` rank and `Navy` note. The abbreviation is preserved, and no
  specialty expansion, naval unit or external namesake is inferred.
- The Library of Congress chronology distinguishes Ray S Cline's junior
  fellowship, Navy cryptanalysis and OSS service. A fellowship is not silently
  converted to employment, and a government assignment is not called a
  civilian employer.
- The DeWitt Clinton roll-list mention occurs in an OSS archive and dates the
  associated photographs, but does not establish what the person did. The
  common search collision with DeWitt Clinton Poole is a different surname.
- Commercial people-finder results for Mary F Clifton included sensitive
  modern information and were rejected without retention.
