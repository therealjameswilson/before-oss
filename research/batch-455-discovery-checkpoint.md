## Emmett F Connelly / Connely evidence

The uncommon indexed Emmett F Connelly is a high-confidence match to the
Detroit figure printed as Emmett F. Connely in contemporary sources. The 22
July 1942 Office of War Information `Information Digest` identifies the
Detroit civilian as nominated to the Army Specialist Corps as chief of field
service with colonel rank. The 1943 `Official Register of the United States`
lists Col. Emmett F. Connely as chief of the Field Office Branch in the Army
Officer Procurement Service. The full name, middle initial, Detroit
association and consistent colonel chronology support the match. No reviewed
source directly crosswalks the index's private identifier, so the one-letter
surname difference remains visible and identity is not promoted to
`confirmed`.

The 1940 `Rand McNally Bankers Directory` lists Emmett F. Connely of Detroit
as president of First of Michigan Corporation. The July 1942 bulletin then
describes him as a civilian entering Army service. First of Michigan is
therefore the last specifically documented civilian employer before wartime
service in the accessible chronology. Because the sources do not explicitly
exclude an intervening 1940-1942 role, the employer claim is medium confidence
with `probable_immediate` temporal basis. It is not labeled the immediate
pre-OSS affiliation. An undated photographic-morgue entry mentioning American
Securities Corporation is retained only as a rejected lead and does not
displace the dated evidence.
