  Schwarzschild** as editor of *Das Neue Tage-Buch* from July 1933 through May
  1940. The index's `Schwarsschild` spelling remains the public heading and the
  identity remains probable until Box 690 is reviewed. The profile preserves a
  date discrepancy with the Leo Baeck Institute summary and does not convert
  1942 radio commentary into an OSS assignment.
- **Hermann C Schwab** and **Charles Schwanda** are probable identities with
  qualified Yale and Wesleyan student affiliations respectively. Neither
  university is presented as an employer or as a proved immediate predecessor
  to OSS.
- Official Army data supports high-confidence identity decisions for **Ernest
  C Schulz**, the second **Albert H Schuster** row, **Jerome C Schuyler**,
  **Walter H Schwab** and **John Schwabauer**, as well as the confirmed Joseph
  R Schurman identity. Army occupation codes remain private and were not

  ambiguous. The remaining unresolved people received explicit archival-review
  outcomes rather than invented employers.
