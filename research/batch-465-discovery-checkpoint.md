Cooper. A Washington Post obituary supplies his full name, Boston birth, New

service in India followed by OSS service in China. Contemporary Authors
independently supplies the full name, matching life dates, the two New York
University degrees, Army enlistment, and assignment with OSS in China.

affiliation with strongly date-bounded temporal evidence and high claim

his doctoral work; New York University is an earlier high-confidence student
affiliation. Neither university is classified as an employer, and no civilian
employer is identified.
