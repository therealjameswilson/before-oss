## Bingham Dai

The 1948 OSS Assessment Staff book explains that the institution printed after
a staff member's name is the institution with which that person was officially
connected before government service. Its staff list gives Bingham Dai, Ph.D.,
with Duke University. This is direct evidence for Duke as Dai's immediate
pre-OSS institutional affiliation and, because the role was teaching faculty,
his last civilian employer.

Appalachian State University's collection guide places Dai on the Peking Union
Medical College faculty in 1935, records his 1939 departure and says he secured
a teaching position at Duke. Geoffrey Blowers's scholarly chronology supports
the Peking appointment, Dai's 1943 Duke psychiatry appointment and his summer
1945 OSS work in Kunming. Peking Union Medical College is therefore retained as
earlier documented prewar employment, not collapsed into the immediate Duke
claim.
