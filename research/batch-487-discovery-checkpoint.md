## Hubert E. Coyer

The Denver Public Library's 10th Mountain Division Name Index matches the
uncommon exact name, middle initial and seven printed digits while supplying
the omitted officer prefix. It identifies Second Lieutenant Hubert E Coyer
with Headquarters, 3rd Battalion, 87th Infantry. The supporting PDF page was
rendered and visually inspected.

The official 1942 New York Adjutant General report independently records Coyer
as a captain in the 74th Regiment, New York Guard, from 3 December 1940 until
honorable discharge on 4 May 1942. A 1931 Rochester city directory identifies
him as a teacher at John Marshall High School. Those pages were also rendered
and checked.

The combined uncommon name, officer-number bridge and state-guard record
support a high-confidence identity and commissioned-Army classification. The
New York Guard service is published at high confidence as a documented prewar
military assignment. The school relationship is qualified medium-confidence,
documented-prewar employment. Neither is labeled immediate or last civilian
because the reviewed sources do not establish the intervening chronology.
